06COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers for venue managers

Frequently asked questions in Juba.

Got questions about how we work in South Sudan? Here is what venue owners and general managers usually ask us.

Why focus so heavily on WhatsApp and phone follow-up in Juba? +

In South Sudan, the overwhelming majority of hospitality inquiries—from corporate conference hall bookings to table reservations and weekend room inquiries—occur via WhatsApp and direct mobile calls. If a customer sees your venue on Facebook or Instagram but faces an unresponsive or unlisted WhatsApp number, they quickly contact another venue. We make sure that handoff is instant and smooth.

Do you work with international NGOs, UN missions, and expat groups? +

Yes. Juba venues serve both local residents and international organizations. We help you present clear, professional specifications (generator reliability, Wi-Fi speeds, conference equipment, and catering packages) that institutional procurement teams require when selecting event or lodging venues.

Do you guarantee room bookings or sales numbers? +

No, and we are candid about this. We guarantee reliable marketing work, high-quality content, accurate profile updates, and fast inquiry response systems. Final customer bookings depend on your venue's pricing, availability, food quality, and front-desk hospitality.

How does content and price approval work before publishing? +

Nothing goes live without your team's direct approval. We submit draft captions, graphics, and price references via WhatsApp or email for your team to check. Once approved, the content is released according to our agreed schedule.

What if our team prefers to communicate primarily on WhatsApp? +

That is standard practice in Juba. Our entire collaboration—from briefing and content review to weekly status check-ins—can be managed seamlessly over WhatsApp or phone calls alongside formal email documentation.
